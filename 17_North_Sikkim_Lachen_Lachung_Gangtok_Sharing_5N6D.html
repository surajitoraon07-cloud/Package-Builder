Morning pickup from Gangtok in a sharing vehicle for Lachen. En route visit 3 major sightseeing points including Singhik View Point, Seven Sisters Waterfalls and Chungthang Confluence. Arrive in Lachen by evening and check in at hotel (as per driver choice / group allotment). Dinner and overnight stay in Lachen.

Day 03: Lachen to Gurudongmar Lake Excursion & Transfer to LachungEXCURSION DAY

Early morning (approx. 4:00 AM) drive to the sacred high-altitude Gurudongmar Lake at 17,800 ft. Marvel at the turquoise waters surrounded by snow peaks. Return to Lachen for lunch, check out and proceed towards Lachung. Check in at Lachung hotel (as per driver choice / group allotment). Dinner and overnight stay in Lachung.

General terms & policies

Permits & ID documents: Original Voter ID card or Passport with 4 passport-size photos per person is mandatory for North Sikkim (Lachen, Gurudongmar Lake, Lachung, Yumthang & Zero Point) permits. Aadhaar cards are not accepted for North Sikkim permits.

Sharing cab arrangement: Transits operated via sharing vehicles run on fixed group schedules. Guests must adhere to the designated departure times at all pickup points.

North Sikkim accommodations: Stay in Gangtok is confirmed at Hotel Norbuling Retreat. Stays in Lachen & Lachung are provided as per driver choice / operator allotment as per standard package norms.

High altitude advice: Gurudongmar Lake (17,800 ft) and Zero Point (15,300 ft) are high-altitude zones. Carry adequate warm clothing and follow safety guidelines.

Weather / landslide: In case of heavy snowfall or landslides blocking routes to Gurudongmar or Zero Point, alternative sightseeing where feasible will be provided. Extra expenses due to force majeure events are to be borne directly by guests.
