Detailed 5-Day Tour Itinerary

Day 01: NJP / IXB Pick-up to Gangtok TransferPRIVATE BOLERO

Pick-up from NJP Railway Station / IXB Bagdogra Airport in a reserved private Bolero cab. Drive along the picturesque Teesta River valley towards Gangtok. Check in at pet-friendly Hotel Sayaju Inn, Gangtok. Evening free to relax or stroll around MG Marg. Overnight stay at Gangtok.

Private Bolero PickupTeesta River ValleySayaju Inn GangtokPet-Friendly Transfer

Day 02: Gangtok to Lachung (North Sikkim) via 5-6 Sightseeing PointsPRIVATE BOLERO

After breakfast, proceed towards Lachung in North Sikkim via private Bolero. En route enjoy 5 to 6 major sightseeing points including Singhik Viewpoint, Seven Sisters Waterfalls, Naga Waterfalls, Chungthang Confluence and the Mangar Homestays area. Check in at pet-friendly Hotel Eagle’s Eye in Lachung. Dinner and overnight stay in Lachung.

Seven Sisters WaterfallsNaga WaterfallsChungthang ConfluenceHotel Eagle’s Eye Lachung

Day 03: Yumthang Valley & Zero Point ExcursionEXCURSION DAY

Early morning excursion to the famous Yumthang Valley (Valley of Flowers at 11,800 ft) and further up to Zero Point (Yume Samdong) at 15,300 ft to experience pristine snow-capped mountains. Return to Lachung for lunch and spend a peaceful evening admiring the snow peaks. Overnight stay at Hotel Eagle’s Eye.

Yumthang Valley ExcursionZero Point (Yume Samdong)Hot Springs ExplorationHotel Eagle’s Eye

Day 04: Lachung to Gangtok Return TransferPRIVATE BOLERO

Enjoy breakfast amidst mountain views before the journey back from Lachung to Gangtok in your private Bolero. En route stop for scenic photo opportunities and lunch. Arrive in Gangtok by late afternoon and check in at Sayaju Inn. Free evening for leisure and local shopping. Overnight stay in Gangtok.

Pet travel policy: The entire trip is arranged in a reserved private Bolero vehicle for comfortable, stress-free travel for your dog. Pet food and specialized care products must be carried by the guest.

North Sikkim permits & ID proofs: Original Voter ID card or Passport with 4 passport-size photographs is mandatory for North Sikkim (Lachung / Yumthang / Zero Point) permits. Aadhaar is not valid for permit issuance.

Zero Point excursion: Zero Point visit is included in the private package, subject to weather, snowfall and permit approval from Army authorities.

Hotel accommodations: Pre-confirmed pet-accessible rooms at Sayaju Inn (Gangtok) and Hotel Eagle’s Eye (Lachung). Check-in 12:00 PM, check-out 10:00 AM.

Weather / landslide clause: In case of severe weather, snow blockages or landslides in North Sikkim, alternate routes / stays will be coordinated; any extra direct expenses will be borne by the guest.
