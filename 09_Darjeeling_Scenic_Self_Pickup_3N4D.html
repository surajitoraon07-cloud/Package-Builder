Detailed 4-Day Tour Itinerary

Self pickup from Gangtok by your exclusive Wagon R cab. Enjoy a scenic drive across the Teesta River valley and lush tea slopes, arriving at Darjeeling (~100 km / 4 hrs). Check-in at Hotel Dev Regency / Dev Perriot. Evening free to stroll around Chowrasta and Mall Road.

Day 02: Tiger Hill Sunrise & Darjeeling 8-Point Sightseeing TourSIGHTSEEING DAY

Early morning (4:00 AM) drive to Tiger Hill for sunrise over Mt. Kanchenjunga. Return via Batasia Loop & Ghoom Monastery. Complete the full Darjeeling 8-Point sightseeing tour in your exclusive Wagon R. Overnight stay at Darjeeling hotel.
