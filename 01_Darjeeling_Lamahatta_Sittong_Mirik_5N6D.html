Detailed 6-Day Tour Itinerary

Day 01: NJP Station / IXB Bagdogra Airport to Darjeeling (Self Pickup)SELF PICKUP

Guests reach Darjeeling on own arrangements (self pickup from NJP / Bagdogra). Arrive in Darjeeling (6,700 ft) and check in at Hotel Dev Regency. Spend the evening strolling around Chowrasta Mall Road and local colonial bakeries. Dinner & overnight stay at Hotel Dev Regency.

Self-transfer to DarjeelingCheck-in at Hotel Dev RegencyEvening at leisure on Mall Road (Chowrasta)Dinner & night stay

Day 02: Tiger Hill Sunrise & Darjeeling Local SightseeingSIGHTSEEING DAY

Early morning (4:00 AM) trip in your Private Wagon R to Tiger Hill for the sunrise over Mount Kanchenjunga. Visit Ghoom Monastery and Batasia Loop & War Memorial. Return to the hotel for breakfast, then proceed for Darjeeling local sightseeing. Dinner & overnight stay at Hotel Dev Regency.

Tiger Hill Kanchenjunga SunriseGhoom MonasteryBatasia LoopHimalayan Mountaineering Institute (HMI)Padmaja Naidu Himalayan ZooDarjeeling Cable Car (Ropeway View)Japanese Peace PagodaTenzing RockHappy Valley Tea Estate View

Day 03: Lamahatta Eco Park, Tinchuley & Takdah Heritage Village ExcursionEXCURSION DAY

Post breakfast, board your Private Wagon R for a scenic day excursion. Visit Lamahatta Eco Park with its pine forest pathway and hilltop lake, proceed to the offbeat village of Tinchuley (mountain viewpoints & organic gardens), and explore Takdah (colonial heritage bungalows, orchid centre & tea gardens). Return to Darjeeling by evening. Dinner & overnight stay at Hotel Dev Regency.

Lamahatta Eco Park & Pine Forest WalkTinchuley Gumbadara ViewpointPeshok Tea Garden VistasTakdah Heritage British Bungalows & Orchid Centre

Day 04: Full-Day Sittong Orange Village & Valley ExcursionEXCURSION DAY

After breakfast, set out in your Private Wagon R towards Sittong, celebrated as the “Orange Village” of North Bengal along the serene valleys of the Kurseong hills. Discover mountain hamlets, old bamboo bridges, the historic Sittong church, orange orchards and the Riyang River bank. Return to Darjeeling in the late afternoon. Dinner & overnight stay at Hotel Dev Regency.

Sittong Orange OrchardsOld Sittong Monastery / ChurchRiyang River Valley viewsAhaldara Viewpoint / Latpanchar viewsLeisure evening in Darjeeling

Day 05: Lepchajagat, Simana Indo-Nepal Viewpoint & Mirik Lake ExcursionEXCURSION DAY

Following breakfast, depart in your Private Wagon R for an excursion along the Mirik ridge. Traverse the pine woods of Lepchajagat, stand at the India-Nepal border at Simana Viewpoint, stop by the border bazaar of Pashupati Nagar, see the slopes of Tingling Tea Garden, and enjoy boating and the footbridge at Mirik Sumendu Lake. Return to Darjeeling for your final evening. Dinner & overnight stay at Hotel Dev Regency.

Lepchajagat Pine ForestSimana Indo-Nepal Border ViewpointPashupati Nagar Market ViewTingling Tea Estate ViewpointMirik Sumendu Lake & Helipad Ground

Day 06: Darjeeling to NJP Railway Station / IXB Bagdogra (Self Drop)SELF DROP

After breakfast, complete check-out formalities at Hotel Dev Regency. Guests depart from Darjeeling on own arrangements (self drop to NJP Railway Station / IXB Bagdogra Airport), taking home cherished memories of the Himalayan valleys.
