Detailed 3-Day Tour Itinerary

Day 01: NJP / IXB Pickup to Gangtok TransferPRIVATE BOLERO

Pick up from NJP Railway Station or IXB Bagdogra Airport in your dedicated Bolero cab and proceed towards Gangtok along the scenic Teesta River valley. Check-in at Hotel Sayaju Inn, Gangtok. Evening free to stroll around MG Marg. Enjoy dinner at the hotel. Overnight stay at Hotel Sayaju Inn.

Bolero Cab NJP PickupTeesta River Scenic DriveHotel Sayaju Inn Check-inMG Marg StrollMAP Meal Plan (Dinner)

Day 02: Excursion to Tsomgo Lake, Baba Mandir & Nathula PassEXCURSION DAY

After breakfast at the hotel, embark on a full-day excursion in your Bolero to Tsomgo (Changu) Lake (12,400 ft), the Baba Harbhajan Singh Mandir (13,200 ft) and the Indo-China border post at Nathula Pass (14,140 ft). Return to Gangtok in the evening. Dinner and overnight stay at Hotel Sayaju Inn.

Day 03: Gangtok to NJP / IXB Departure DropPRIVATE BOLERO

Enjoy breakfast at Hotel Sayaju Inn. Complete check-out formalities and board your Bolero cab for the return transfer to NJP Railway Station or IXB Bagdogra Airport, concluding your Sikkim holiday with sweet memories.
