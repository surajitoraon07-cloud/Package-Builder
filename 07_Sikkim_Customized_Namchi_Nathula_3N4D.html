Terms & Conditions and Booking Policies

General terms & policies

Vehicle & transport mode: Day 2 sightseeing is operated by a Private WagonR exclusively for your group of 4 adults. Day 3 high-altitude excursion is operated on a sharing cab basis with permit assistance. Day 1 pick-up and Day 4 drop are self-arranged by guests.

Point-to-point service: Private and sharing hill vehicles operate strictly point-to-point as per the fixed itinerary and are not on disposal.

Permit requirements: Tsomgo Lake, Baba Mandir and Nathula Pass require official military permits. Carry Original Voter ID Card or valid Indian Passport with 4 passport-size photographs per person. Aadhaar / PAN Card are not accepted.

Nathula Pass permits: Issued by the Indian Army subject to weather, road connectivity and daily quota. In case of sudden border closure, alternative sightseeing will be arranged.
