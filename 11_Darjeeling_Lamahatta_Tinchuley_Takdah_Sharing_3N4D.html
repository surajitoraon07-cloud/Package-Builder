Detailed 4-Day Tour Itinerary

Day 01: NJP Station / IXB Airport Pick-up to Darjeeling TransferSHARING CAB

Warm welcome at NJP Railway Station / IXB Bagdogra Airport by our representative. Board your sharing cab and proceed towards Darjeeling through scenic mountain landscapes. Check-in at Hotel Dev Regency. Free evening to relax or stroll around Mall Road and Chowrasta. Overnight stay at Hotel Dev Regency (MAP Plan).

Day 02: Tiger Hill Sunrise & 9-Point Darjeeling Local SightseeingSIGHTSEEING DAY

Early morning (4:00 AM) trip to Tiger Hill for Mount Kanchenjunga sunrise, followed by Ghoom Monastery & Batasia Loop. Post breakfast, full 9-point Darjeeling local sightseeing: HMI, Padmaja Naidu Zoological Park, Cable Car Ropeway view, Japanese Temple, Peace Pagoda, Tenzing Rock, Gombu Rock, Tibetan Refugee Self Help Center & Tea Garden view. Overnight stay at Hotel Dev Regency (MAP Plan).

Day 03: Lamahatta Eco Park, Tinchuley & Takdah Day ExcursionEXCURSION DAY

Post breakfast, depart in your sharing cab for an offbeat day excursion. Visit Lamahatta Eco Park & Pine Forest, Tinchuley View Point & Monastery, Takdah Heritage Bungalows, Orchid Center and Peshok Tea Garden Viewpoint (Lover’s Meet View Point). Return to Darjeeling in the evening. Dinner & overnight stay at Hotel Dev Regency (MAP Plan).

Day 04: Darjeeling to NJP / IXB Drop via 7 Sightseeing PointsSHARING CAB

After breakfast, check out from Hotel Dev Regency. Board your sharing cab for a scenic downhill transfer to NJP Railway Station / IXB Airport via 7 sightseeing points: Mirik Sumendu Lake, Pashupati Nagar (Nepal Border Market), Simana Viewpoint, Tingling Tea Garden Viewpoint, Lepchajagat Pine Forest, Mirik Monastery & Swiss Cottage Eco-Zone. Drop at station / airport for your onward journey.
