Detailed 5-Day Tour Itinerary

Day 01: NJP Railway Station / IXB Airport Pick-up to GangtokPRIVATE INNOVA CRYSTA

Pick-up from NJP Railway Station / IXB Airport. Board your dedicated private Innova Crysta and drive to Gangtok (~120 km / 4.5 hrs) along the Teesta River. Upon arrival, check in at Hotel Sayaju Inn. Evening free to explore M.G. Marg for local shopping and food. Overnight stay at Gangtok.

NJP / IXB Pick-up (Private Innova Crysta)Teesta River Scenic DriveHotel Sayaju Inn Check-inM.G. Marg Evening

Day 02: Gangtok to Lachung Scenic Drive via 5 PointsPRIVATE INNOVA CRYSTA

Morning pick-up in your Private Innova Crysta from your hotel. Proceed to Lachung (~125 km / 6 hrs) through scenic North Sikkim landscapes with 5-point sightseeing including Seven Sisters Waterfalls, Singhik Viewpoint, Mangan Valley, Bhim Nala Waterfalls & Chungthang Confluence. Check-in at Hotel Eagle’s Eye (Lachung). Dinner & overnight stay at Lachung.

Private Innova Crysta CabHotel Eagle’s Eye Stay5-Point SightseeingSeven Sisters & Bhim Nala Falls

Day 03: Yumthang Valley & Zero Point Excursion, Return to GangtokEXCURSION DAY

Early morning excursion to Yumthang Valley (Valley of Flowers, 11,800 ft) and Zero Point (Yumesamdong, 15,300 ft). Experience snow vistas and mountain hot springs. (Permits / Zero Point local fees payable extra.) Return to Lachung for lunch, then proceed back to Gangtok. Check in at Hotel Sayaju Inn for overnight stay.

Private Innova Crysta CabYumthang Valley ExcursionZero Point (Permit Cost Extra)

Day 04: Gangtok Free Day / Leisure & ShoppingLEISURE DAY

Post breakfast, enjoy a relaxed leisure day in Gangtok. Explore local cafes, shop for Sikkimese handicrafts at Lal Bazaar or M.G. Marg, or take an optional local city excursion. Dinner & overnight stay at Hotel Sayaju Inn.

Breakfast at HotelLeisure at M.G. Marg & Local MarketsOvernight Stay at Hotel Sayaju Inn

Day 05: Gangtok Hotel Check-out & Departure Transfer to NJP / IXBPRIVATE INNOVA CRYSTA

After breakfast, check out from Hotel Sayaju Inn. Board your private Innova Crysta for the downhill scenic transfer to NJP Railway Station / IXB Bagdogra Airport, concluding your North Sikkim & Gangtok trip.
