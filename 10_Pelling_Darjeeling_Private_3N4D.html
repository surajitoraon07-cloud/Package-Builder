Detailed 4-Day Tour Itinerary

Day 01: NJP Railway Station Pick-up & Transfer to PellingPRIVATE CAB

Arrival at NJP Railway Station / IXB Airport. Meet our representative and transfer in your reserved private vehicle to Pelling, West Sikkim (7,200 ft) past scenic Teesta Valley and mountain slopes. Check in at hotel on arrival. Evening free to relax and enjoy panoramic Kanchenjunga views. Overnight stay in Pelling.

NJP / IXB Pick-upScenic Sikkim DrivePelling Hotel Check-in

Day 02: Pelling Local Sightseeing & Transfer to DarjeelingEXCURSION DAY

After breakfast, explore Pelling highlights including Pelling Skywalk, Chenrezig Statue, Khecheopalri Sacred Lake, Rimbi Waterfalls and Kanchenjunga Falls. Post sightseeing, take the scenic transfer drive to Darjeeling (6,700 ft). Check in at your Darjeeling hotel. Overnight stay in Darjeeling.

Pelling Glass SkywalkKhecheopalri LakeKanchenjunga WaterfallsTransfer to Darjeeling

Day 03: Tiger Hill Sunrise & Darjeeling Local Points SightseeingSIGHTSEEING DAY

Early morning (approx. 4:00 AM) drive to Tiger Hill for sunrise over Mt. Kanchenjunga. Visit Ghoom Monastery and Batasia Loop on return. Post breakfast, cover local sightseeing: HMI, Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoo, Tenzing & Gombu Rock, Tibetan Refugee Self-Help Center, Tea Garden Viewpoint and Peace Pagoda. Overnight stay in Darjeeling.

Tiger Hill SunriseBatasia Loop & GhoomHMI & ZooPeace Pagoda

Day 04: Darjeeling to NJP Railway Station Drop via 7 Points SightseeingPRIVATE CAB

Post breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed for the return drop to NJP Station / IXB Airport via the 7-point route covering Mirik Sumendu Lake, Pashupati Nagar (Indo-Nepal Border), Simana Viewpoint, Gopaldhara Tea Estate and Kurseong Valley View. Conclude your tour with drop at NJP / IXB.

Mirik LakeIndo-Nepal Border MarketGopaldhara Tea GardensNJP / IXB Drop
