Detailed 3-Day Tour Itinerary

Day 01: NJP to Darjeeling Scenic Bike Ride via Mirik & Pine ForestsBIKE RIDE

Receive your rental bike at the NJP Railway Station / IXB Bagdogra area. Embark on a self-ride towards Darjeeling via the scenic Mirik route: Sumendu Lake, pine forests and rolling tea gardens along the Indo-Nepal border road. Arrive in Darjeeling (6,710 ft), check in at hotel and stroll Chowrasta Mall Road in the evening. Dinner & overnight stay in Darjeeling (MAP Plan).

Bike Handover at NJPScenic Ride via Mirik LakeIndo-Nepal Border Pine RouteHotel Check-in & Mall Road Evening

Day 02: Full 10-Point Darjeeling Sightseeing Ride & Tiger Hill ExcursionSIGHTSEEING DAY

Early morning ride to Tiger Hill (8,400 ft) for the sunrise over Mount Kanchenjunga, visiting Ghoom Monastery and Batasia Loop on the way back. Post breakfast, gear up for a 10-point local sightseeing ride covering the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute (HMI), Padmaja Naidu Zoo, Ropeway Point, Peace Pagoda, Japanese Temple, Tibetan Self-Help Center, Tenzing Rock and Happy Valley Tea Estate. Dinner & overnight stay.

Tiger Hill Kanchenjunga SunriseGhoom Monastery & Batasia LoopHMI, Zoo & Peace PagodaTea Gardens & Tenzing Rock

Day 03: Darjeeling to NJP Downhill Bike Ride via Kurseong & DropBIKE RETURN

Post breakfast, check out from the hotel. Mount your bike for a downhill ride back towards NJP via Kurseong valley with views of Margaret’s Hope Tea Estate, Kurseong Tourist Lodge and the Rohini zigzag roads. Complete bike return formalities at NJP Railway Station / IXB for your onward journey.
