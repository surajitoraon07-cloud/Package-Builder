Detailed 6-Day Tour Itinerary

Day 01: NJP Railway Station / IXB Pick-up & Scenic Transfer to GangtokPRIVATE CAB

Arrival at NJP Railway Station / IXB Airport. Warm welcome by our representative and transfer in a private reserved vehicle to Gangtok (5,500 ft, ~120 km / 4.5 hrs) alongside the Teesta River. On arrival, check in at hotel. Evening free to stroll down M.G. Marg for shopping and local delicacies. Overnight stay at Gangtok.

NJP / IXB Pick-upTeesta River DriveGangtok Hotel Check-inM.G. Marg Leisure

Day 02: Full-Day Excursion to Tsomgo (Changu) Lake & Baba MandirEXCURSION DAY

After breakfast, embark on a full-day excursion to high-altitude Tsomgo Lake (Changu Lake) at 12,400 ft, a sacred glacial lake surrounded by snow peaks. Continue to Baba Harbhajan Singh Memorial Mandir (13,200 ft). Return to Gangtok by late afternoon. Overnight stay at Gangtok.

Tsomgo (Changu) LakeBaba Harbhajan Singh MandirKyongnosla Alpine Sanctuary Route

Day 03: Gangtok 10-Point Full-Day Local Sightseeing TourSIGHTSEEING DAY

Comprehensive 10-point local sightseeing covering Gangtok’s main attractions: Bakthang Waterfalls, Tashi Viewpoint (Kanchenjunga view), Ganesh Tok, Hanuman Tok, Directorate of Handicraft & Handloom, Flower Show (Ridge Park), Enchey Monastery, Gangtok Ropeway (Cable Car), Do Drul Chorten Stupa and Namgyal Institute of Tibetology. Evening free. Overnight stay at Gangtok.

Bakthang FallsTashi ViewpointGanesh & Hanuman TokRopeway Cable CarFlower ShowDo Drul Chorten

Day 04: Gangtok to Darjeeling Scenic Mountain TransferTRANSFER DAY

After breakfast, check out from Gangtok hotel and drive to Darjeeling (6,700 ft, ~100 km / 4 hrs) through tea garden estates and pine valleys. Upon arrival, check in at hotel. Rest of the day free to explore Chowrasta Mall Road and local markets. Overnight stay at Darjeeling.

Inter-State Mountain DriveDarjeeling Hotel Check-inChowrasta & Mall Road

Day 05: Darjeeling Full-Day Local Sightseeing (Without Tiger Hill)SIGHTSEEING DAY

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast followed by full-day local sightseeing (excluding early morning Tiger Hill): Batasia Loop & War Memorial, Ghoom Monastery, Himalayan Mountaineering Institute (HMI), Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park, Tenzing & Gombu Rock, Tibetan Refugee Self-Help Center, Happy Valley Tea Garden Viewpoint, and Peace Pagoda & Japanese Temple. Overnight stay at Darjeeling.

Batasia LoopGhoom MonasteryHMI & Padmaja ZooTenzing RockHappy Valley Tea GardenPeace Pagoda

Day 06: Darjeeling to NJP Railway Station Drop via 7 En-Route PointsPRIVATE CAB

Post breakfast, check out and start the return trip to NJP Station / IXB Airport via the 7-point en-route route: Mirik Sumendu Lake, Pashupati Nagar Market (Indo-Nepal Border), Simana Viewpoint, Gopaldhara Tea Estate, Kurseong Pine Valley View, Rohini / Pankhabari Viewpoint and Sukna Forest Belt. On-time drop at NJP / IXB.

Mirik LakePashupati Market (Nepal Border)Simana ViewpointGopaldhara Tea GardenNJP / IXB Drop
