Detailed 5-Day Tour Itinerary

Day 01: NJP Station / IXB Airport Pick-up & Transfer to Zuluk / PadamchenPRIVATE CAB

Upon arrival at NJP Station / Bagdogra Airport (IXB), meet our representative and board your dedicated private cab for a scenic drive along Rongli and the Silk Route into Padamchen / Zuluk (7,000-9,400 ft). Check-in at Pradhan’s Homestay, relax and soak in the village atmosphere and mountain views.

Homestay Check-in: Pradhan’s HomestayDedicated Private CabAPAI Plan (Lunch & Dinner Included)

Day 02: Zuluk & Silk Route Local SightseeingSIGHTSEEING DAY

Early morning visit to Thambi Viewpoint (11,200 ft) for a panoramic sunrise over Kanchenjunga and the iconic 32 hairpin bends of Zuluk. Continue to Lungthung, Kuekhola Waterfalls, Zuluk Park and Laxmi Chowk. Return to Pradhan’s Homestay for evening tea and homestay hospitality.

Thambi Viewpoint SunriseSilk Route Hairpin BendsKuekhola FallsAPAI All Meals Included

Day 03: Zuluk to Gangtok via Baba Mandir, Tsomgo Lake & Nathula PassEXCURSION DAY

After breakfast, check out and drive along the high-altitude Silk Route passing Gnathang Valley, Old Baba Mandir, Elephant Lake (Kupup) and Tukla Valley. Visit Nathula Pass (14,140 ft) on the Indo-China border, then proceed to the glacial Tsomgo Lake (12,400 ft). Arrive in Gangtok and check in at Hotel Norbuling Retreat.

Hotel Check-in: Hotel Norbuling RetreatGnathang & Old Baba MandirTsomgo Lake & NathulaDinner Included

Day 04: Gangtok Local Sightseeing HighlightsSIGHTSEEING DAY

After breakfast, enjoy a full-day city sightseeing tour of Gangtok: Rumtek Monastery or Ranka Monastery, Banjhakri Waterfalls, Do Drul Chorten Stupa, Namgyal Institute of Tibetology, Flower Exhibition Centre and Tashi View Point. Return to Hotel Norbuling Retreat and explore MG Marg in the evening.

Gangtok Local SightseeingBanjhakri Falls & MonasteriesTashi ViewpointBreakfast & Dinner Included

Day 05: Gangtok to NJP Railway Station / IXB Airport Drop TransferPRIVATE CAB

Enjoy breakfast at Hotel Norbuling Retreat, complete check-out and board your private cab for the return drive down to NJP Station / Bagdogra Airport (IXB) for your onward journey with unforgettable memories of the Silk Route.
