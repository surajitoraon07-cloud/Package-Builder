Detailed 4-Day Tour Itinerary

Day 01: NJP Railway Station / IXB Airport Pick-up & Transfer to PellingPRIVATE WAGON R

Arrival at NJP Railway Station / IXB Airport. Board your dedicated Wagon R for a scenic drive along the Teesta and Rangit rivers into West Sikkim. Arrive at Pelling (7,200 ft) with panoramic views of the Kanchenjunga range. Check in at Norbulingka Retreat. Evening free to relax or explore the local hill town market. Overnight stay at Norbulingka Retreat, Pelling.

NJP Pick-upScenic River DriveNorbulingka Retreat Check-in

Day 02: Pelling Full Day Local Sightseeing (8 Points incl. Glass Skywalk)SIGHTSEEING DAY

After breakfast, set out on a comprehensive 8-point sightseeing tour in your dedicated Wagon R: Pelling Glass Skywalk & Chenrezig Statue, Pemayangtse Monastery, Rabdentse Ruins, Rimbi Waterfalls, Rimbi Orange Garden, Khecheopalri Sacred Lake, Kanchenjunga Waterfalls and Helipad Viewpoint. Overnight stay at Norbulingka Retreat.

Pelling Glass SkywalkKhecheopalri LakeKanchenjunga WaterfallsPemayangtse Monastery

Day 03: Excursion: Ravangla (Buddha Park), Namchi & Temi Tea GardenEXCURSION DAY

After breakfast, proceed on a full-day excursion. Visit Ravangla Buddha Park with the giant 130 ft Sakyamuni Buddha statue surrounded by snow peaks. Head to Temi Tea Garden (Sikkim’s only tea estate) for valley views. Continue to Namchi Siddheshwar Dham (Char Dham) and Samdruptse Hill before returning to Pelling. Overnight stay at Norbulingka Retreat.

Buddha Park RavanglaTemi Tea GardenNamchi Char Dham

Day 04: Pelling to NJP Railway Station / IXB Airport DropPRIVATE WAGON R

Enjoy your final breakfast at Norbulingka Retreat. Board your Wagon R for the return journey to NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport with cherished memories of West Sikkim.
